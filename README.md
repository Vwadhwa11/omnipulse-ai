# OmniPulse AI — Coming Soon Landing Page

A sleek, responsive "Coming Soon" landing page for **OmniPulse AI**, the unified AI-powered marketing platform that consolidates email automation, performance analytics, influencer outreach, and social media campaign management into a single autonomous engine.

---

## 🚀 Live Local Server

The website is currently built and served locally at:
👉 **[http://localhost:5173/](http://localhost:5173/)**

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **React 19**
- **Vite 8**
- **Tailwind CSS v4** (with custom glassmorphism and glow animations)
- **Lucide Icons**

---

## ✨ Features Included

1. **High-Converting Hero Section**:
   - Live **Launch Countdown Timer** (Days, Hours, Minutes, Seconds)
   - Real-time **VIP Waitlist Registration** with:
     - Instant queue position calculator (`#4,821`)
     - Unique shareable referral link generator + copy-to-clipboard
     - Role picker (Founder, Growth Marketer, Agency Director, E-commerce, Creator)
   - Integration ribbon for Meta Ads, Google Ads, Shopify, TikTok, Klaviyo, Stripe, etc.

2. **The 4 Core Platform Pillars**:
   - **Autonomous Email Marketing**: AI copywriting, dynamic lifecycle drip funnels, inbox reputation guardian.
   - **Digital Performance Analytics**: First-party cookieless attribution, predictive ROAS & CAC forecasting, autonomous budget reallocation.
   - **Influencer Discovery & Outreach**: 20M+ verified creator search, fake follower detection, automated contextual outreach pitch generation.
   - **Unified Social Media Suite**: Cross-network publishing, AI asset repurposing (1 blog -> 20 platform-tailored posts), optimal timing queue.

3. **Live Interactive Product Sandbox**:
   - Users can test-drive simulated AI executions:
     - **Email Tab**: Toggle between Cart Recovery, Dormant Win-back, and Launch announcements to see real-time synthesized email copies and predictive open rates.
     - **Analytics Tab**: Live ROAS radar with channel-by-channel breakdown and AI optimization insights.
     - **Influencer Tab**: Explore vetted creator profiles and trigger 1-click tailored pitch generation.
     - **Social Tab**: View how one core announcement is adapted for LinkedIn, X (Twitter), and Instagram.

4. **Dynamic ROI & Time-Savings Calculator**:
   - Interactive sliders for monthly marketing spend and team size.
   - Real-time projections for hours saved per week, SaaS subscription cost savings, and revenue lift.

5. **Legacy Stack vs. OmniPulse AI Comparison Table**:
   - Contrasting fragmented tool chaos ($1,850+/mo across 6 tools) vs. a unified AI operating system.

6. **Early Adopter VIP Perks & Tiers**:
   - Pioneer Cohort (3 months free Pro, 50% lifetime discount, 1:1 setup call).
   - Innovator Cohort.
   - General Public launch roadmap.

7. **FAQ Accordion & Clean Footer**:
   - Clear answers on private beta rollout, privacy/security (SOC2/GDPR), integrations, and agency multi-tenancy.
